Privacy Policy for Uppy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

This policy explains what information Uppy ("the game", "we", "us") collects and how it is used. Uppy is developed by Tom Elders Ltd.

Contact: hello@tomelders.com

Summary

  • Uppy has no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking.
  • We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.
  • Game progress and settings are stored on your device.
  • If you make an in-app purchase, the purchase is processed by Apple or Google, and verified through our purchase provider, RevenueCat.

Information stored on your device

Uppy saves your progress, settings, and unlocked themes locally on your device. This data stays on your device. We do not receive it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

Information collected through in-app purchases

Uppy offers optional in-app purchases (for example, themes). To manage purchases we use RevenueCat (https://www.revenuecat.com), a third-party service that verifies purchases and tracks which items you own.

When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat may receive:

  • An anonymous app user ID generated by RevenueCat
  • Purchase and subscription details (product purchased, date, store receipt/token)
  • Device and app information (platform, OS version, app version, country/region, IP address)

This information is used only to confirm your purchases, unlock purchased content, and restore purchases on a new device. We do not receive your payment details. Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. RevenueCat's privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

Apple's and Google's own privacy policies apply to your use of their stores:

  • Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Information we do not collect

We do not collect or share: your name, email address, precise or approximate location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, advertising identifiers, or health data. We do not show ads. We do not sell your data.

Children's privacy

Uppy does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age in your region).

Data retention and deletion

Local game data is deleted when you uninstall the game. Purchase records held by RevenueCat are kept as needed to provide purchase restoration and to meet legal obligations. To request deletion of data associated with your anonymous purchase ID, contact us at hello@tomelders.com and we will submit the request to RevenueCat.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold, or to object to its processing. Contact us at hello@tomelders.com to exercise these rights.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date above will change when we do. Material changes will be noted in the game's store listing or release notes.
